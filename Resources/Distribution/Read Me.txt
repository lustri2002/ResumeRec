ResumeRec — Preview Beta

REQUIREMENTS
Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 26 or later.

INSTALL
1. Quit any older copy of ResumeRec or Pausa. Save an active recording first.
2. Drag ResumeRec.app onto the Applications folder shortcut.
3. Eject this disk image, then open ResumeRec from Applications.
4. ResumeRec lives in the menu bar. Open Settings from its menu.

THIS BETA'S SIGNATURE
This preview uses a local ad hoc signature. It is not signed with an Apple
Developer ID and has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may block the first
launch after a download. Only proceed if you trust the source of this copy.
After trying to open the app, use System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Open Anyway, if offered by macOS, and confirm that you want to open ResumeRec.
On a managed Mac this option may be unavailable; contact your administrator.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

FIRST RECORDING
1. In Settings > Output, choose a writable folder for your recordings.
2. In Recording, choose a display, window or region and the audio you need.
3. In Webcam, optionally enable the camera and customize its appearance.
4. Start Recording, then use Pause / Resume and Stop & Save from the menu bar.
   Shortcuts are configurable in Settings > Controls.
5. Wait for saving to finish. Your MP4 appears in the selected folder.

PERMISSIONS
Allow screen recording when macOS asks. If access is not yet available, use
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording.
Quit and reopen ResumeRec if requested by macOS. Camera and microphone access
are requested when those features are enabled. Granting screen access does
not automatically start a recording: return to the app and try again.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Source and device lists refresh when their dropdown opens.
- The floating webcam preview shows the actual recording size and position.
- Hiding the preview does not remove the webcam from the saved video.
- Pause removes the paused time from the final video. You can change sources
  while paused, but the recording's video dimensions remain fixed.
- ResumeRec works offline. It has no account, telemetry or automatic updater.
- Temporary session files are stored alongside recordings. If saving fails,
  keep the hidden .ResumeRec-session folder and use Output > Recover a Session.
  Session folders from earlier Pausa versions are also supported.

UPDATES AND FEEDBACK
For an update, save your recording, quit the app, and replace ResumeRec in
Applications with the newer version. Settings are retained with this build's
existing app identifier. Send feedback to the person who gave you this beta,
including the app version, macOS version, Mac model, settings and steps to
reproduce the issue. Include recording content only if you choose to share it.

REMOVE
Quit ResumeRec and move the app from Applications to the Trash.
Your saved recordings are separate files and are not removed with the app.
